  • Perfect dash macro

    Jump, dash, turn-to-torso, dash. Every timing is exposed and tunable; one key fires the whole thing frame-consistently.

  • Face lock

    Hold a key to stay squared onto the nearest target in range. The range is yours to set.

  • Alignment auto-Q

    Watches your X/Z alignment and fires Q inside a window and threshold you define.

  • Dash cancel

    Cuts the dash after a set delay, with optional momentum kill for a hard stop.

  • Macro editor

    Build your own sequences from M1, keys, dashes, turns, jumps and waits. Save them, run them.

  • Weakest-dummy detect

    Auto-targets the lowest-health dummy so training reps go where they count.
